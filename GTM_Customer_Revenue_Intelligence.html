Business Problem
GTM changes across segments, products, pricing, and customer motions can shift renewal risk and revenue exposure, but leadership needs a fast way to understand where those changes are creating risk, which customer/product cohorts are most affected, and where intervention should be prioritized.
Analytical Approach
Analyze attrition exposure across segments, industries, products, geographies, adoption levels, and renewal timing to identify how customer and GTM signals intersect and highlight priority cohorts for action.
Stakeholder Outcome
An exec can go from "we have a renewals problem" to "here are the 74 low-adoption Retail accounts driving it, and the three plays to run" in a few clicks, cutting the manual pull-and-reformat cycle that usually precedes a business review.
